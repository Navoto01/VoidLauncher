## ✨ Overview

**VoidLauncher** is a premium, flagship Android launcher crafted to redefine mobile aesthetics, tactile interaction, and spatial personalization. Built by solo developer **Navoto01** under the **AnonLab** brand, VoidLauncher delivers a futuristic user experience that blends real-time **Liquid Glass glassmorphism**, mathematical **Monarch G2 squircle geometry**, an innovative **4-way 2D free app resizing** canvas, an integrated **AI Assistant**, and an advanced **3D Spatial Parallax Wallpaper Engine**.

Whether you want a hyper-minimalist setup, an expressive glassmorphic interface, or a high-efficiency productivity workspace, VoidLauncher adapts effortlessly with zero-compromise performance.

---

## 🎨 Design Language & Visual Engine

### Liquid Glass Aesthetic & Real-Time Shaders
- **Real-Time Frosted Glass Shaders:** Experience true translucent materials with hardware-accelerated background blur, vibrancy saturation boosting, and optical lens refraction.
- **Adaptive Luminance System:** Elements dynamically analyze the luminance of your wallpaper underneath to automatically adjust between frosted glass, smoked dark acrylic, and high-contrast tinting.
- **Dynamic Weather Overlays:** Live atmospheric effects such as customizable rain particle overlays interact with your homescreen canvas.

### Monarch G2 Squircle Geometry
- **Mathematical Continuous Curvature:** Replaces harsh circular cutouts and standard rounded rectangles with organic G2 squircles for a unified aesthetic.
- **Adaptive Corner Locking:** Standard 1x1 app icons use an organic 27% Monarch curvature. When expanded into multi-span cards (e.g., 2x1, 2x2, 3x2), the corners smoothly interpolate and lock to an exact, refined **20dp** radius to maintain perfect balance across the grid.

### Fluid Spring Physics & Natural Motion
- **Momentum & Elastic Springs:** Every swipe, bounce, sheet reveal, and app launch responds with natural mass, damping, and velocity.
- **Scroll-End Haptic Engine:** Subtle, satisfying haptic clicks when reaching edge limits, snapping grid items, or triggering multi-app selection.
- **Seamless App Launch Transitions:** Apps smoothly expand directly out of their homescreen icons with matched geometry and depth.

## 📱 Core Launcher Experience

### Homescreen & Dynamic Grid Canvas
- **Flexible Grid System:** Customize column counts, row counts, page margins, and cell spacing to achieve your desired layout density.
- **Elastic Overscroll:** Fluid rubber-banding when scrolling through workspace pages.
- **Multi-Span Element Support:** Mix and match standard apps, wide widgets, expanded app cards, and shortcuts freely on the same page.

### Floating Liquid Glass Dock
- **Elevated Glass Pill:** Houses your most important applications in a floating translucent bar with real-time blur.
- **Granular Customization:** Adjust dock elevation, horizontal/vertical padding, icon size scaling, and label visibility.
- **Dynamic Accent Tinting:** Harmonizes the dock's background color with your active wallpaper's dominant palette.

### Quick Search Box (QSB) & Built-in AI Assistant
- **Dock or Top Placement:** Position the quick search bar at the bottom for easy one-handed reach or at the top of your screen.
- **Integrated AI Assistant:** An intelligent conversational AI is built directly into the launcher. Tap the search bar or trigger a gesture to ask questions, summarize content, generate ideas, or control tasks without opening external apps.
- **Universal Device Search:** Instant search across installed applications, contacts, device settings, shortcuts, and web search engines (Google, DuckDuckGo, Brave, etc.).

### Smart App Drawer & Categorization
- **Alphabetical Fast-Scroll Index:** Quickly jump through apps with smooth typography and haptic feedback along the scrollbar.
- **Category Tabs & Work Profiles:** Organize apps into customizable category tabs or separate work and personal profiles.
- **Instant Keyboard Popup:** Option to immediately open the search keyboard upon swiping up into the drawer.

## 🛠️ Edit Mode & Next-Gen Canvas Customization

Long-press on empty canvas space or pinch inward to enter **Edit Mode**, transforming your homescreen into an interactive editing suite:

### 4-Way 2D Free App Resizing
VoidLauncher introduces direct on-screen app resizing:
- **Direct 4-Directional Drag:** Grab any edge or corner to resize app cards in real-time horizontally (left/right) or vertically (up/down).
- **Responsive Vector Artwork:** Expanded apps become dynamic cards (1x1, 2x1, 1x2, 2x2, 3x2, 2x3, 4x2) with vector graphics and backgrounds that adapt to the new aspect ratio.

### Multi-App Selection & Batch Drag
- **Visual Selection Arc & Checkmark:** Highlight apps with glowing Monarch selection rings.
- **Simultaneous Multi-Drag:** Select multiple apps across different pages and move them together in one fluid drag gesture.
- **Instant Folder Grouping:** Drop multiple selected apps onto each other to instantly generate a folder.

### Edit Mode Quick Toolbar (The 4 Pillars)
When entering Edit Mode, the bottom dock transforms into four dedicated creator tools:

1. **🎨 Wallpaper Studio:** Open the Wallpaper Manager, explore curated collections, or create a 3D Spatial Wallpaper.
2. **🛠️ System Developer & DPI Tools:** Live DPI resolution overrides, layout bounds inspectors, and real-time frame rate monitors.
3. **🧩 2-Pane Widget Selector:** Browse installed widgets with full live previews, Monarch squircle clipping, and zero-margin padding toggles.
4. **⚙️ Launcher Settings:** One-tap shortcut straight into the comprehensive customization settings.

## 🖼️ Spatial & Depth 3D Wallpaper Studio

VoidLauncher includes a built-in 3D Spatial Wallpaper engine that brings depth to your screen:

### 3D Parallax Spatial Wallpapers
- **Instant Depth Estimation:** Converts ordinary 2D photos into multi-layered 3D spatial wallpapers.
- **Gyroscope-Driven Parallax:** Layers shift and react in real-time to your phone's tilt and physical movement.
- **Spatial Creator Studio:** Interactively adjust layer depth sensitivity, focal plane positions, and layer scaling with live 3D preview.

### Dual Wallpaper Engine (Lockscreen vs Homescreen)
- **Independent Targeting:** Apply completely different static or 3D spatial wallpapers to your Lockscreen and Homescreen.
- **Synchronized Unlock Transitions:** Seamless crossfade when unlocking your device from the lockscreen to the homescreen.
- **Material You Dynamic Theming:** Automatically extracts harmonic color accents from your active wallpaper to tint icons, widgets, and Liquid Glass surfaces.

### Curated Wallpaper Collections & Theming
- Access an expanding gallery of cloud-updated and offline wallpapers spanning minimalist, abstract, spatial, and landscape styles.

## 🔒 Lockscreen, Control Center & Privacy Vault

### Integrated Custom Lockscreen
- **Aesthetic Clock Typography:** Choose from multiple modern clock faces, date layouts, and font weights.
- **Lockscreen Widgets & Shortcuts:** Add quick glanceable widgets and customize bottom corner shortcut toggles (flashlight, camera, AI).
- **Passcode & PIN Protection:** Secure your lockscreen and homescreen editing modes with PIN or biometric authentication.

### Private Space & Hidden Apps Vault
- **App Encryption & Stealth Mode:** Hide sensitive applications from the app drawer behind a secure PIN or biometric lock.
- **Folder PIN Locking:** Lock individual homescreen folders to protect personal content from unauthorized access.

### Control Center & Quick Gestures
- **Custom Gesture Actions:** Configure swipe down, swipe up, double-tap, and pinch gestures to trigger device functions, open the Control Center, lock the screen, or launch AI.
